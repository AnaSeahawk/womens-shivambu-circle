Women’s Shivambu Circle

The circle

Shivambu as lived experience.

A gathering for women to speak carefully and honestly about Shivambu as lived experience. The circle holds questions, discoveries and shared witness while each woman’s experience remains her own.

How we sit together

  • No one is required to share.
  • No one is required to agree.
  • No one is required to know.
  • We speak from the “I.”
  • We listen with care.
  • We leave room for mystery.
  • Every woman remains sovereign in her own body.

Curiosity is welcome. Listening is welcome. Passing is welcome.
